# Screen references

The images in this folder are **design references, not screenshots of Mori**.
They were generated from the prompts in
[docs/product-screen-prompts.md](../../product-screen-prompts.md) during
planning in 2026-05, before most of the screens existed.

- Many show features that are planned or were dropped, such as account sign-in,
  plans and cloud connectors. Mori has no account and no cloud sync. Which
  screens are implemented is tracked in
  [docs/product-screen-reference.md](../../product-screen-reference.md).
- Every name, face, project and number in them is invented.
- Logos of other products appear as illustration only. They are trademarks of
  their owners, who do not endorse Mori.
